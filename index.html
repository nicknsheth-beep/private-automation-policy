HomeAssistant 2852

Private account and home automation operated by Nick Sheth. This application connects the operator's authorized Google accounts to personal productivity and home-automation tools. It is not a public sign-up service.

Privacy policy

This policy applies to Google user data accessed by HomeAssistant 2852 and its private automation integrations.

Data accessed and purposes

Depending on permissions granted and the requested task, the application may access account identity, email messages and attachments, calendar events, contacts, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, and forms and responses. Where a connected home integration is authorized, it may also access permitted device information. Access is used to perform authorized tasks such as finding information, summarizing messages, preparing or sending communications, managing documents and calendars, processing forms, and operating home integrations. Granting a permission does not mean every category is used for every task.

Processing and sharing

Data is processed on the operator's devices and through connected services needed to carry out the authorized task. Relevant content may be sent to configured AI or automation providers for processing, and communications or files may be shared with recipients selected by the operator. Those providers also have their own privacy policies. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Access is limited to the operator and service providers involved in the authorized functionality, except where required by law or necessary for security.

Storage, security, and retention

OAuth credentials, working files, task outputs, and operational records may be retained in the operator's configured systems. Credentials are not published with this policy. Access controls and appropriate safeguards are used; no system can guarantee absolute security. Data is retained as needed for authorized functionality and the operator's records, until deleted by the operator or on an applicable deletion request. Revoking Google access stops new API access but does not automatically delete previously saved files, task outputs, logs, or backups. Copies held by connected providers are subject to their retention policies.

Control and deletion

The operator can disconnect the application at Google Account connections, remove stored OAuth credentials, and delete locally retained data and task records. Requests about access, correction, or deletion can be sent to nicknsheth@gmail.com. Backups and records required by law may need separate handling.

Google API Limited Use

HomeAssistant 2852's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
